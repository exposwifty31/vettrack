# Model Council Synthesis — Making the VetTrack TV Board Modern & Professional

Council members: **GPT-5.6 Sol** and **Claude Fable 5**. Both independently analyzed the screenshot of the Hebrew RTL critical-equipment board (vettrack.uk/board) with the TV-screen context in mind.

## Where Models Disagree

| Topic | GPT-5.6 Sol | Claude Fable 5 | Why They Differ |
|-------|-------------|----------------|-----------------|
| The "17 הצעות ממתינות לאישור" banner | Demote to a subtle utility chip or remove it from the board — it's an administrative queue, not an equipment-health signal, and its prominence weakens the clinical hierarchy | Promote it to the board's loudest element — a ~140 px hero number in the state strip, because it's the single most actionable datum on screen | GPT-5.6 Sol optimizes for clinical-safety hierarchy (equipment state first); Claude Fable 5 optimizes for actionability (the only item that changes anyone's next action) |
| All-clear presentation | Calm mode: a compact, restrained success panel — green must not flood the screen so that exceptions stand out by contrast | Celebratory full-stage composition: 160–200 px check emblem plus a tinted state strip so the global state is readable peripherally from 8 m without reading | Different theories of glanceability: emphasis conservation for incident detection vs. calm-technology peripheral color-temperature signaling |
| View rotation & ambient motion | Never rotate pages, auto-scroll, or pulse anything; the only motion is a one-time 250–400 ms fade on state change | Rotate secondary views every 45–60 s and add a per-poll "heartbeat" dot — motion signals liveness and doubles as burn-in relief | GPT-5.6 Sol reads W3C pause/stop/hide conservatively (no controls on a TV → no persistent motion); Claude Fable 5 weights liveness signaling and OLED burn-in mitigation higher |
| Staff sign-in list (אחראים) | Compress to one line ("אחראי משמרת: לא סומן") or remove from the wall board entirely if not operationally required | Keep it, but aggregate to "0/5 סומנו" with a segmented progress bar plus names at 32 px | Different assumptions about whether individual sign-in status drives action at the TV vs. only at the console |
| Contrast target | WCAG AA (4.5:1) as the requirement, with his proposed palette landing at 6.3–16:1 | WCAG AAA (7:1) as the explicit target, because TV panels in Vivid/Eco modes and ambient glare erode nominal contrast | Different evidence weighting: spec minimums vs. field conditions of uncalibrated consumer panels |

## Unique Discoveries

| Model | Unique Finding | Why It Matters |
|-------|----------------|----------------|
| Claude Fable 5 | Hebrew is an exception within RTL: [Material 3](https://m3.material.io/foundations/layout/bidirectionality-rtl) specifies linear progress indicators should stay LTR in Hebrew, unlike most RTL languages | The proposed 0/5 sign-in bar and any uptime strip must fill left-to-right even on this fully RTL board |
| Claude Fable 5 | The red "0" in the חשמל card is semantically backwards — zero alerts is good news and should be neutral/green; red must be reserved for active problems ([colorfyi](https://colorfyi.com/blog/color-in-dashboard-design/)) | A board that shows red when nothing is wrong trains staff to ignore red |
| Claude Fable 5 | Hebrew needs more size headroom than Latin at the same distance: no capitals, and letterforms differ in fine interior details (ד/ר, ה/ח, ו/ז) ([JetRuby RTL guide](https://jetruby.com/blog/creating-an-impressive-right-to-left-design/)) | Latin-derived TV type floors should be nudged up one step for Hebrew |
| GPT-5.6 Sol | A connection state machine with hysteresis (live → delayed → stale → offline), keeping last-known values labeled "מצב אחרון ידוע" instead of resetting to zeros | Zeroing on disconnect falsely suggests all issues cleared — a dangerous failure mode for a clinic |
| GPT-5.6 Sol | `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums` on all counts and the clock so updating digits don't jitter; never count-animate numbers because intermediate values are false | Small typographic detail that separates purpose-built displays from web pages |
| GPT-5.6 Sol | Distinguish three timestamps in the data model — equipment event time, last successful heartbeat, and screen render time; only the first two determine trust ([IBM](https://www.ibm.com/think/topics/stale-data)) | "עודכן 10:18" as render time can mask a dead data feed |
| GPT-5.6 Sol | Physically calibrate the TV: disable Vivid mode, sharpening, motion smoothing, and dynamic contrast; validate the design on the actual mounted panel from the farthest viewing point | The rendered design is only half the display pipeline on consumer TVs |

## Comprehensive Analysis

The two models converge with unusual force on the diagnosis: this is a desktop web app projected onto a TV, and the highest-leverage fixes are structural, not cosmetic. Both independently arrived at the same top-priority list — kiosk mode, a viewing-distance type scale with a ~28 px floor and hero-sized numbers, an elevation-based dark palette instead of black-on-black, semantic status color with icon redundancy, and a promoted data-freshness indicator. That convergence, reached from different source sets (GPT-5.6 Sol leaning on Apple/Android/W3C platform specs, Claude Fable 5 on digital-signage distance math and wallboard practice), makes these near-certain wins you can implement without further debate.

The most consequential convergence is also the least visual: both models flagged the on-screen contradiction between "כל הציוד הקריטי זמין" in the center and "אין ציוד קריטי מוגדר" in the rail. Claude Fable 5 framed it sharply as "a configuration failure masquerading as vacuously-true good news"; GPT-5.6 Sol generalized it into a principle of state provenance — every positive claim must show its evidence ("12 מתוך 12 · נבדק כעת"). Both insist the board be modeled as an explicit state machine (healthy / unconfigured / no-data / alert) with distinct full-screen presentations, because a blank database must never be indistinguishable from a healthy clinic. For a veterinary clinic, this is a safety fix disguised as a design fix, and it should be the first engineering task.

The genuine disagreements cluster around one philosophical split: how loud should a wall display be in its normal state? GPT-5.6 Sol argues for "calm mode, incident mode" — normality as a neutral, restrained baseline so that color, space, and motion are conserved for exceptions, with zero persistent animation. Claude Fable 5 argues the opposite corner: the all-clear state is the board's product 95% of the time and deserves a confident, peripherally-readable composition, with a heartbeat dot and slow view rotation to signal liveness and fight burn-in. These aren't contradictory so much as differently weighted: GPT-5.6 Sol prioritizes exception detection, Claude Fable 5 prioritizes ambient trust ("is this screen even alive?"). A defensible middle path takes Claude Fable 5's tinted state strip and evidence tiles for peripheral readability, GPT-5.6 Sol's ban on decorative motion in the main stage, and Claude Fable 5's freshness heartbeat as the single sanctioned ambient signal.

The sharpest single disagreement is the "17 approvals" banner. Claude Fable 5 would make it the hero of the screen; GPT-5.6 Sol would remove it from the board. The resolution depends on a product question only the team can answer: can someone walking past the TV actually act on approvals promptly? If yes, Claude Fable 5's hero treatment is right; if approvals happen at a desk anyway, GPT-5.6 Sol's argument — that an administrative queue dilutes a clinical-safety display — should win. Similarly, on staff sign-ins, Claude Fable 5's "0/5 סומנו" aggregate is the safer default since it preserves the information at readable scale, with GPT-5.6 Sol's removal as the fallback if sign-in status never drives action at the wall.

The unique findings are worth adopting wholesale, since none conflict: Claude Fable 5's Hebrew-specific rules (LTR progress fill, extra size headroom, no letter-spacing or synthetic italics, Heebo/Assistant stack) and its catch of the semantically backwards red "0"; GPT-5.6 Sol's engineering rigor around temporal integrity — the hysteresis connection state machine, "last known state" labeling, three distinct timestamps, and tabular numerals. Between them, on contrast, prefer Claude Fable 5's 7:1 target: it costs nothing in a dark theme and buys margin against uncalibrated panels and corridor glare.

**Recommendation:** implement in this order — (1) kiosk mode on a dedicated account; (2) fix the state logic and build the four designed full-screen states with freshness escalation and last-known-state handling; (3) rebuild the type scale (28 px floor, 64 px headlines, 120+ px heroes, Heebo, tabular numerals) and the elevation-gray palette at 7:1; (4) restructure into the three-band RTL layout both models describe (status band top, state-dependent main stage, compact ops rail/band) with collapsed zero-counters and the 0/5 sign-in aggregate; (5) decide the approvals-banner question against your actual workflow; (6) finish with burn-in hygiene (calibration, night dimming, safe margins) and validate by standing at the far end of the corridor: a 2–3 second glance must answer "is anything wrong, and is anything waiting for me?"
